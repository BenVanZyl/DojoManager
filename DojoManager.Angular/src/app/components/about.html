About Us

We offer traditional Karate training in the Shotokan style under the guidance of the Japan Karate Association, headquartered in Japan, and JKA KDA chief instructor, Jim Wood MBE Shihan (7th Dan), a high ranking member of the Japan Karate Association.

We cater for the age groups of 10 years to infinity.
